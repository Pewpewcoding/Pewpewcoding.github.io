ABOUT SIYIDE

A railway supplier with integrated technical service capability

Liaoning Siyide combines equipment supply, component support, maintenance capability and international business for railway and urban rail customers.

Liaoning Siyide Transportation Equipment Co., Ltd. was established on April 28, 2017 and is registered in the Shenyang Area of the China (Liaoning) Pilot Free Trade Zone. Its operating address is in Tiexi District, Shenyang, within one of Northeast China’s core rail-transit equipment manufacturing areas.

The affiliated company, Shenyang Xinyishunda Rail Transit Equipment Co., Ltd., has been engaged in railway rolling stock and urban rail equipment parts sales, maintenance and after-sales service since April 2013. Together, the two companies form an integrated supply and technical-service chain.

FEATURED SYSTEMS

Key product categories for railway and urban rail vehicles

Core product areas include bogie components, hydraulic dampers, braking systems, couplers, wheelsets, electrical connectors and passenger-coach auxiliary equipment.

01 / BOGIE

Bogie Components

For mainstream bogies including 25G, 25K and 25T passenger coaches, covering frames, axle boxes, positioning devices and foundation brake equipment.

02 / DAMPING

Hydraulic Dampers

Modular twin-tube dampers in light, medium and heavy specifications for primary vertical, secondary vertical, secondary lateral and anti-hunting applications.

03 / ELECTRICAL

Electrical Control & Connectors

Power, control and heavy-duty railway-vehicle connectors, including circular and rectangular configurations.

Brake Systems & PartsBrake valves, cylinders, brake beam assemblies, safety hangers, brake shoes and brake pads.
Couplers & Draft GearCouplers, draft gear and related components for multiple locomotive and rolling-stock types.
Wheelsets & AxlesFor passenger and freight rolling stock, metro and light rail vehicles.
Passenger Coach Auxiliary EquipmentLighting, power boxes, fan parts, piping, kitchen and sanitary equipment, and vacuum toilet systems.
